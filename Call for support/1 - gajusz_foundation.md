# Gajusz Foundation - Awakening Hope When It Is Fast Asleep

*"Where hope ends, the Gajusz Foundation begins. Professionally and expertly, we awaken hope when it is fast asleep."*


**The Gajusz Foundation (Fundacja Gajusz)** is one of the most well-known and highly respected public benefit organizations in Łódź, dedicated to supporting chronically and terminally ill children and their families. The organization has been active since 1998, providing professional medical and psychological help where the medical hope for a cure ends, and the need for a dignified life full of love and safety begins.

## The Story Behind the Foundation

The Gajusz Foundation was born from a personal experience and a mother's promise. In 1998, Tisa Żawrocka-Kwiatkowska was keeping vigil by the bed of her nearly 8-month-old son, Gajusz, in the oncology ward of a hospital in Łódź. The child's condition was very severe, and doctors were preparing the mother for the worst. At that moment, she made a promise: if her son survived, she would establish a foundation to help sick and lonely children. Gajusz miraculously recovered, and Tisa kept her word – this is how the Gajusz Foundation was born.

## The Organization's Mission

The foundation's mission is to provide children with a sense of security, round-the-clock and comprehensive care, and to ensure that they do not lose their childhood during the difficult time of illness. The organization not only treats physical pain but also provides psychological, social, and spiritual care. It employs a team of specialists—doctors, nurses, psychologists, physiotherapists, educators—and cooperates with a multitude of dedicated volunteers to support around 1,000 families each year.

## Main Areas of Activity

The foundation operates on multiple tracks, adapting to the diverse needs of the youngest patients and their families:

### 1\. Inpatient Children's Hospice (The "Palace")

This is a unique place on the map of Łódź – a home for terminally ill children who, for various reasons, cannot stay with their biological families (e.g., due to orphanhood or a complicated home situation). The Palace is not a hospital, but a colorful, warm home where the focus is on the quality of life and making dreams come true, so that every day of the charges is as good as possible.

### 2\. Home Hospice

A team of specialists (doctors, nurses, psychologists) travels to the homes of chronically ill children. Thanks to this, little patients can stay in their own safe environment, surrounded by their loved ones, while receiving fully professional medical help.

### 3\. Perinatal Hospice

Support for parents who learn about a lethal defect of their unborn child while still pregnant. The foundation accompanies them from the moment of diagnosis, through childbirth, up to the time of mourning. It provides consultations with a psychologist, medical specialists, as well as spiritual and organizational support (e.g., commemorative photo sessions, organizing baptism).

### 4\. "Tuli Luli" Pre-adoption Intervention Center

The first center in the Łódź region for infants abandoned by their biological parents. Newborns arrive there directly from hospitals before their legal situation is resolved. The center focuses not only on care but also on "cuddling" (tulenie) – satisfying the natural need for closeness and love, which prevents hospitalism (anaclitic depression).

### 5\. "CUKINIA" Center for Therapy and Help for the Child and Family

A place where entire families receive professional support – including children harmed by fate, struggling with trauma, adaptation difficulties, or emotional problems. This support is dedicated to, among others, adoptive and foster families.

### 6\. Support on Oncology Wards

Volunteers and specialists from the foundation also support patients in children's oncology wards, offering psychological support for parents, art therapy, play, and social assistance. They provide children with an opportunity to relax and take their minds off the hospital reality.

## Why Support the Foundation?

* **Comprehensive Care:** The organization addresses not just medical needs, but the emotional, social, and spiritual well-being of the entire family.
* **Protecting Childhood:** Through its unique facilities and dedicated staff, the foundation ensures that severe illness does not rob children of their right to play, warmth, and love.
* **Decades of Trust:** With over 25 years of experience, the foundation effectively manages complex interventions, providing essential help to around 1,000 families annually.
