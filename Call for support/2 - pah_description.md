# Polish Humanitarian Action (PAH) - Empowering People in Crises

*"Helping people in crises regain control over their lives and their dignity."*


**Polish Humanitarian Action (Polska Akcja Humanitarna - PAH)** is one of the most trusted and experienced non-governmental organizations in Europe. Founded in 1992, PAH provides life-saving assistance and long-term development aid to people affected by armed conflicts, natural disasters, and extreme poverty across the globe.

## The Organization's Mission

The mission of PAH is to make the world a better place by alleviating human suffering and promoting humanitarian values. The organization believes in global solidarity and the fundamental right of every human being to a dignified life.

## Core Areas of Action

PAH operates in some of the most challenging regions in the world, focusing on both immediate relief and sustainable solutions:

### 1\. Water, Sanitation, and Hygiene (WASH)

Access to clean water changes everything. PAH is renowned for its global efforts in fighting water poverty. The organization constructs deep water wells, rehabilitates damaged water networks, and builds sanitary facilities in regions affected by severe droughts and conflicts, particularly in countries across Africa and the Middle East.

### 2\. Emergency Humanitarian Relief

When a sudden disaster strikes—whether it is an earthquake, flood, or the outbreak of war—PAH is there to provide immediate assistance. They supply food packages, hygiene kits, safe shelter, and medical supplies to those who have lost everything. The organization actively responds to major global crises, including the ongoing war in Ukraine.

### 3\. Education and Psychosocial Support

PAH believes that education cannot wait, even in emergencies. They help rebuild schools, provide educational materials, and offer crucial psychosocial support to children traumatized by war and displacement, helping them regain a sense of safety and normalcy.

### 4\. Food Security and Livelihoods

Beyond immediate food aid, PAH helps communities rebuild their independence. By providing seeds, agricultural tools, and vocational training, they empower local populations to cultivate their own food, rebuild their businesses, and sustain their livelihoods long-term.

## Why Support PAH?

* **Proven Track Record:** Over 30 years of effective, transparent humanitarian work in dozens of countries worldwide.
* **Direct Impact:** PAH operates directly on the ground, ensuring that aid reaches the most vulnerable populations swiftly and efficiently.
* **Building Resilience:** They do not just deliver temporary aid; they work *with* local communities to build their resilience so they can thrive independently in the future.
